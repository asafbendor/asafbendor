## 🚀 About Me

- 🧠 **From teaching AI to building with it.** For years I taught people how to use AI. Today I mostly build with it: every project here goes from idea to launch in weeks, driven by a small team of AI agents.
- 📱 **Mobile, shipped to real stores.** Live products across Google Play and the App Store, cross platform from a single Expo codebase, Hebrew RTL first.
- ⚡ **Edge native by default.** Cloudflare Workers, D1 and R2 carry my sites and APIs. No servers to babysit, no cold starts, near zero cost.
- 🤖 **Autonomous agents in production.** Content pipelines, a daily AI persona, marketing automation and a WhatsApp assistant, all running unattended on cron.
- 🎨 **One design language across everything.** A single versioned product-language contract governs colors, RTL copy and button lexicon for every app, site and dashboard I own.
- 📊 **Instrumented, not guessed.** Custom dashboards pull live Play Console, App Store Connect and AdMob data, so product decisions come from numbers.
