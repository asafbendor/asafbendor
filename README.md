## 🚀 About Me

- 🧠 **From teaching AI to building with it.** For years I taught people how to use AI. Today I mostly build with it: every product below went from idea to launch in weeks, driven by a small team of AI agents.
- 📱 **Six apps live on the stores.** Five of them on Google Play and the App Store both, cross platform from a single Expo codebase, Hebrew RTL first, up to seven languages each.
- ⚡ **Edge native by default.** Cloudflare Workers, D1 and R2 carry my sites and APIs. No servers to babysit, no cold starts, near zero cost.
- 🤖 **Autonomous agents in production.** An AI persona that has published 185 posts in 100 days untouched, a WhatsApp assistant, a trading research system, and the blog pipeline that writes my own site.
- 🎮 **Games and social products that ship fast.** A music game covered by Mako, a World Cup prediction league, a daily browser arcade, a daily news guess.
- 🎨 **One design language across everything.** A single versioned product-language contract governs colors, RTL copy and button lexicon for every app, site and dashboard I own.
- 📊 **Instrumented, not guessed.** Custom dashboards pull live Play Console, App Store Connect and AdMob data, so product decisions come from numbers.
